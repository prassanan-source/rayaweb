# PT Bank Raya Indonesia Tbk. (PT Bank Raya Indonesia Tbk)

PT Bank Raya Indonesia Tbk. is a Banking company. Bank Raya is a digital bank part of the bri group that provides solutions for various industries. PT Bank Raya Indonesia Tbk. employs 742 people (+6.9% YoY, +78 people), founded in 1989. Headquartered in Jakarta Selatan, DKI Jakarta, Indonesia, with presence in United States. Its workforce is distributed across Indonesia and Malaysia.

## About

PT Bank Raya Indonesia Tbk was founded by the Plantation Pension Fund (Dapenbun) on 27th September 1989 under the name PT Bank AGRO. On 11 December 1989, BRI Agro acquired a business license from the Minister of Finance and launched commercial operations on 8 February 1990.
In 2003, the Bank took a new degree in its journey by becoming a Public Company after having made an initial public offering on the Surabaya Stock Exchange. At the same time, the Bank changed its name to PT Bank Agroniaga Tbk. In 2007, the Bank’s shares with the AGRO stock code had been listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange. In 2006, Bank AGRO upgraded its rank to a Foreign Exchange Commercial Bank.

On 3 March 2011, the Deed of Acquisition of Shares of PT Bank Agroniaga Tbk was signed between PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (Persero) Tbk (BRI) and Dapenbun in Jakarta. In 2012, BRI had officially become the Controlling Shareholder of PT Bank Agroniaga Tbk alongside its 23rd birthday, Bank AGRO changed its name to PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia Agroniaga Tbk, with the commercial name BRI Agro.

BRI Agro has increasingly taken steps with BRI to serve digital ecosystem by launched it's first digital loan called PINANG on 2019. To demonstrate its seriousness, the Company continues to synergize with BRI.

Since 2021, Bank Raya has transformed into a Digital Bank as appointed by BRI as the Main Digital Bank that Provides Digital Financial Solutions with the Widest Access for the Indonesian People.
